# A PM who keeps the to-do list

2026-09-25. Built for v0.17.0.

## Why

The assistant ordered each project's to-do list, from a chat that is about everything else too. With planning in place, the planners know what each task changes, but nobody looked across the tasks: which should go first, and what should wait for what. The owner wanted that to sit with a team member, as "a regular role the other team members have, rather than a hierarchy", so the team stays flat.

## What was built

- **PM is a role kind, like planner.** A member can hold `pm` alongside other roles, or on its own. No template has a PM; `set_team`'s `pm_member` (and the Team tab) adds one. A member who already fills a seat takes the PM role in that seat, and one who doesn't gets a seat of its own.
- **When it looks.** A project with a PM is marked `pm_due` when work is queued, planned or finished, or when the owner answers the PM. The loop gives the PM one read-only turn before starting the next task. The turn has no repository access: its prompt carries the brief, and every unfinished task with its plan and what it waits for.
- **What it decides.** Its reply sets:
  - the order of the queued tasks;
  - the full list of what any unfinished task waits for.
  Both are checked like any other change: the same project, no loops, and exactly the queued tasks. A bad part is dropped and the rest applies. The change and the PM's one-line note go to the project's activity as "task.ordered".
- **Who overrules it.** `ordered_by` records who last set the order: `owner`, `assistant` or `pm`. After the owner or the assistant orders the list, their order holds for every task that was there then. The PM may only place tasks queued since.
- **Questions** come to the owner as a `pm-question` decision, with "Use your judgment" and "Keep the order as it is" as choices, or an answer in the owner's words. The PM doesn't look again while a question is open; the answer brings it back with the owner's words.
- **When it can't look.** A reply that still can't be read after one retry, or an engine error, marks the list looked at and says so in the activity. Work never waits on the PM. A usage hold on the PM's engine only postpones the look.

## What it never does

It doesn't plan, build, review or land, and it doesn't direct the other seats. A message can't reach it, since it isn't part of any task.

## Not built

- Suggesting splits, and noticing overlap between plans beyond ordering them.
- Which free seat picks up what. That comes with parallel work.
